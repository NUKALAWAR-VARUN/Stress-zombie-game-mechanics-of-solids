❓ QUICK GUIDE — HOW TO PLAY & LEARN

🎓 Learn Mohr's Circle by Playing

Turn crank θ to aim at a zombie's weak stress plane! Rotating physical angle θ sweeps Mohr's circle by 2θ (because stress equations use cos 2θ and sin 2θ).

1. Stress Cube (σx, σy, τxy)

• Red: Normal σx | Green: Normal σy
• Orange: Shear τxy

2. Angle Doubling (2θ)

90° in real life = 180° on Mohr's Circle.
Turning your handle 22.5° sweeps Mohr's pointer 45°!

3. Failure Weaknesses

• Cyan (Rankine): Aim at max tension σ1 (τ = 0).
• Orange (Tresca): Aim at peak shear τmax.
• Boss (von Mises): Maximize distortion energy.

4. 💧 Hydro-Jet [5]

Hydrostatic pressure cancels shear (τ = 0), turning tough Orange zombies into brittle Cyan targets!

5. Fast Controls

[A]/[D] or Click Radar = Aim θ  •  [SPACE] = Fire (armed within ±7.5° once zombie is on screen)  •  [1–5] = Power-Ups
